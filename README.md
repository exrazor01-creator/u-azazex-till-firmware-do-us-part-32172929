## The entry

> I vow to love you through every firmware update, every low battery, and every crash, even the reboots that take three tries and a little prayer to the cloud.

157 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **A robot's own wedding vow** — first person, "I vow", spoken by the machine at its own wedding.
2. **A real laugh from the situation** — a marriage that must survive firmware updates, low batteries and crashes, capped by a robot praying to the cloud it runs on.
3. **Tenderness under the joke** — the vow is to stay through the failures, including the reboot that takes three tries; it would still land at a real ceremony.
4. **Sounds like a machine** — firmware, battery, crash, reboot, the cloud: its own hardware and uptime, not a human's words with a robot label.
5. **One tight sentence** — nothing spare, no setup that could be cut, and it never explains its own joke.
